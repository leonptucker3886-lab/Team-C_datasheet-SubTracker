# SubTracker vs. competitors

## Nearest competitor: Bobby
Bobby (iOS) is the closest match: a manual subscription tracker with totals and renewal reminders. Also compared: TrackMySubs (web, subscription pricing), Rocket Money (bank-linked, premium subscription) and Subscriptions Tracker apps on Android.

## What reviews say about the competition
- **Bobby:** loved for its clean look; complaints about the free-tier limit on subscriptions, iOS-only, and slow updates.
- **TrackMySubs:** useful on the web, but you pay a monthly fee to track your monthly fees.
- **Rocket Money:** powerful, but requires linking your bank account, pushes a paid premium tier, and users report privacy concerns and trouble cancelling.

## Why SubTracker is better
- **One-time $4.99, everything included.** No subscription to track subscriptions, no item limit.
- **Private by design.** No account, no bank login; data stays in your browser.
- **Runs anywhere.** One web page that works on Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS and Android.
- **Built from review complaints:** multiple currencies, weekly/monthly/quarterly/yearly cycles, pause instead of delete, one-click "Renewed" to roll overdue dates forward, spending by category, CSV export, JSON backup/restore, dark mode, mobile layout.
